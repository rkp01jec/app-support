Find a little inspiration in a daily prompt, draw at your own pace, and keep your sketches in a personal gallery.

Getting started

Draw on the canvas using your finger or Apple Pencil. Choose a color, brush size, or drawing tool. Use Undo to reverse the last stroke or Clear to start over. Tap Save today's sketch to add it to your gallery. From a saved sketch, you can share or delete it.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my sketches saved?

Sketch images and gallery details are stored locally in the app on your device. They appear in Gallery after a successful save.

Can I share a sketch?

Open a sketch in Gallery and tap Share to use the iOS share sheet. You choose whether to send or save the image with another app.

Can I edit a saved sketch?

Saved sketches are image snapshots. You can view, share, or delete them from Gallery; the original drawing remains on the drawing canvas until you clear it or leave the screen.

How can I report a problem?

Contact support through the repository issues page. Mention Daily Sketch Pad and include your device, iOS version, steps to reproduce, and what happened. Do not attach private drawings unless you intend to share them publicly.

Privacy

Sketches and gallery information stay on your device unless you choose to share an image using the iOS share sheet. The app does not use advertising or analytics. Read the Daily Sketch Pad Privacy Policy.
