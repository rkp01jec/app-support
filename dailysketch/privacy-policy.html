Daily Sketch Pad

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This policy describes information handled by Daily Sketch Pad, a daily drawing prompt and sketchbook app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your saved sketch images, their prompts and dates, and a sketch-save count locally on your device using Apple's standard app storage. The developer does not receive this locally stored information. Device backups may include app data according to your device settings.

Sharing

If you choose Share for a sketch, the app passes that image to the iOS share sheet. You choose whether to send or save it using another app or service. The privacy practices of the destination you choose are governed by that provider.

Analytics, advertising, and other data

The app does not include advertising or analytics services and does not operate its own account or data-collection service. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Data removal

You can delete individual sketches from Gallery. Removing the app or its local data may remove remaining app data. Device backups are managed through your Apple device settings.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. For privacy or support questions, contact us through the support repository and mention Daily Sketch Pad. Please do not include sensitive information.
