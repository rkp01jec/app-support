Grade1Math

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Grade1Math is an educational math-practice app for first-grade learners. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information collected

Grade1Math does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The app does not require an account, login, name, email address, or internet connection.

Local progress

The app saves practice totals, correct answers, and the best quiz score locally on the device using Apple’s UserDefaults. This information is not sent to us or shared with third parties. Removing the app or clearing its data removes the locally stored progress.

Speech and device access

Optional spoken prompts and answer choices use Apple’s on-device or system speech features. Grade1Math does not access contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone recordings, health data, or files outside the app’s own storage.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

Grade1Math does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, data brokerage, remote APIs, or other external services.

Children’s privacy

Grade1Math is designed for first-grade math practice and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, visit the public support issues page and mention Grade1Math. Please do not include private or sensitive information.
