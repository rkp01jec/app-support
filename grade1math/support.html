Math practice that keeps learning focused.

Grade1Math is a small SwiftUI app for first-grade math practice, with immediate feedback, spoken prompts, and simple progress tracking.

What the app includes

The app is designed for short, encouraging practice sessions on iPhone and iPad running iOS 16 or later.

Practice your way

Choose mixed practice, addition, subtraction, or comparing numbers. Questions are generated as you go.

Helpful feedback

Answer choices are followed by immediate feedback and a brief explanation. Optional speech can read questions and choices aloud.

See your progress

The home screen shows correct answers, questions answered, best quiz score, and overall accuracy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start practicing?

Open Grade1Math and tap Start Practice. Use the category menu during a session to switch between practice types.

Can the app read questions aloud?

Yes. Spoken questions and answer choices are enabled by default and can be turned off in the category menu.

How can I report a problem?

Please open an issue in the support repository and mention Grade1Math. Include what you expected, what happened, and the device or iOS version if relevant. Do not include private information.

Does the app need an internet connection?

The app has no external services or package dependencies. Practice content is generated in the app, and progress is saved locally.

Privacy and data

Grade1Math does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or external services. The app stores total correct answers, total answered, and best quiz score locally with Apple’s UserDefaults. That progress stays on the device unless you remove the app or clear its data. Read the full privacy policy.
