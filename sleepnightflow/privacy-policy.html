SleepNightFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Overview

SleepNightFlow is a standalone sleep and ambient-sound app. We respect your privacy.

Information We Collect

SleepNightFlow does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or internet connection.

Audio and Device Data

Audio is played locally on your device using bundled sound files. The app does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or health data.

Analytics and Advertising

SleepNightFlow does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, or data brokerage.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit SleepNightFlow Issues.
