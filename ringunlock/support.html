Support

Spin each ring, line its gap up with the marker, and unlock the next level.

How to play

Drag a ring in a circular motion to rotate it. Line every gap up with the marker at the top; a gap snaps into place when it is close. Later levels introduce rings that spin in reverse or move as mirrored pairs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate a ring?

Touch a ring and drag around its center. Each ring can be moved independently unless it is paired with another ring on a later level.

Why does one ring move the opposite way?

From level 4, dashed rings rotate in the opposite direction to your drag. From level 7, dotted rings may be linked; rotating one moves its partner the other way.

How are my progress and score saved?

Your current level and score are saved locally on your device. If you use Game Center, leaderboard scores are submitted to Apple.

How can I report a problem?

Contact support through the repository issues page. Mention RingUnlock and include your device and iOS version, the steps to reproduce the issue, and what happened. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Privacy

The game stores level progress and score on your device. It does not use ads or analytics. If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple handles your player identity and any leaderboard submissions under its own privacy terms. Read the RingUnlock Privacy Policy.
