Family Chores & Stars · Family chore tracker

Support

Make everyday chores rewarding. Track family tasks, celebrate progress, and redeem stars for rewards.

Getting started

Open the Family tab to add family members. On Today, add chores with the + button. Tap a chore to choose who completed it and award its stars. Add family rewards from the Rewards tab; each member can claim a reward when they have enough stars.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add chores and rewards?

Use the + button on Today to add a chore, or on Rewards to add a reward. Chores can be worth one to three stars; reward costs can be set in increments of five.

How do I undo a chore?

Tap the checkmark on a completed chore to undo it for today. The chore can then be completed again.

Where is family information stored?

Family member names, chores, rewards, and activity records are stored on this device. The app does not sync them to a developer-operated account or server.

How can I report a problem?

Contact support through the repository issues page. Mention Family Chores & Stars and include your device, iOS version, steps to reproduce, and what happened. Avoid sharing children's personal details or other sensitive information.

Privacy

The app stores family information and activity locally on your device. It does not include advertising, analytics, accounts, or third-party data services. Read the Family Chores & Stars Privacy Policy.
