Family Chores & Stars

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This policy describes information handled by Family Chores & Stars, a family chore and rewards app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores family member names and avatars, chore and reward details, and chore and reward activity on your device using Apple's standard app storage. This information is used to display family profiles, star balances, and activity in the app. It is not sent to a developer-operated server. Device backups may include app data according to your device settings.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for family use and lets a family enter member names. These details remain in the app's local storage; the app does not provide an account service or transmit them to the developer. Please avoid entering information that is not needed to use the app.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use third-party accounts or data services. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Data removal

Removing the app or its local data may remove information stored by the app. Any device backups are managed through your Apple device settings.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. For privacy or support questions, contact us through the support repository and mention Family Chores & Stars. Do not include children's personal details or other sensitive information.
