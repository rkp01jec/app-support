BlockBloom

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

BlockBloom is a block puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app stores and what may be handled by Apple Game Center.

Information collected by BlockBloom

BlockBloom does not operate its own account system, analytics, advertising, or data collection service. The app does not send your local game progress or player name to the developer.

Scores and player name stored locally

The app saves best scores, local leaderboard entries (including the name you enter, score, game mode, and date), and your saved player name on your device using Apple’s UserDefaults. This local information is not synced by BlockBloom. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove the saved information.

Apple Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, BlockBloom uses Apple’s GameKit to authenticate with Apple and submit completed game scores to the configured Classic or Daily leaderboard. Apple handles Game Center player identity, leaderboard features, and related data under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. Game Center is an Apple service, not a service operated by the BlockBloom developer. You can manage Game Center sign-in and settings through your Apple device settings.

Other device data

BlockBloom does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone recordings, health data, or files outside the app’s own storage.

Children’s privacy

BlockBloom is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Any data handling associated with Game Center is subject to Apple’s policies and the account’s settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, visit the support issues page and mention BlockBloom. Please do not include private or sensitive information.
