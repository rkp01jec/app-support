What the game includes

BlockBloom combines familiar block-placement puzzle play with daily challenges and a calm, colorful presentation.

Two ways to play

Classic is endless play with random pieces. Daily Bloom gives everyone the same piece sequence for that calendar day.

Clear and score

Complete a row, column, or 3×3 box to clear it. Streaks add score multipliers as you keep clearing.

Keep your best blooms

Top scores are saved on your device. Apple Game Center leaderboards are available when configured and signed in.

Frequently asked questions

How do I place a piece?

Touch and drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Release it when the highlighted cells show a valid placement.

How do clears work?

Fill a complete row, column, or 3×3 box to clear its blocks. Clearing multiple lines or boxes together can earn more points.

How does Daily Bloom work?

Daily Bloom uses the device calendar date to generate that day's piece sequence. Starting another game on the same day uses the same sequence.

Where are my scores saved?

Your local Top Blooms entries, player name, and best scores are stored on your device. They are not synced by the local leaderboard.

How can I report a problem?

Open an issue in the support repository and mention BlockBloom. Include what you expected, what happened, and your device or iOS version if relevant. Do not include private information.

Privacy and data

BlockBloom stores local scores and your chosen player name on your device. It has no advertising or analytics. If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple may process your player identity and leaderboard scores under its own terms and privacy policy. Read the full privacy policy.
