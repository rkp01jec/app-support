Nonogram Daily · Picture-logic puzzle

Support

Solve a new 10×10 picture-logic puzzle each day and keep your streak going.

How to play

Row and column clues show the lengths of consecutive filled groups. Tap a cell to fill it or mark it as empty with X mode. A wrong fill costs one life; complete all required filled cells to solve the puzzle.

Frequently asked questions

What do the clues mean?

Each number gives the length of a consecutive run of filled cells in that row or column. Separate runs must have at least one empty cell between them.

What happens when I lose all my lives?

The current attempt ends. You can restart and try the same daily puzzle again.

Is the daily puzzle the same for everyone?

The puzzle is generated on your device from the calendar date. Devices using the same date generate the same daily puzzle.

Where are my streak and solve time saved?

Your streak, best streak, total solved, best time, and last solved date are saved locally on your device. If you use Game Center, leaderboard scores are submitted to Apple.

How can I report a problem?

Contact support through the repository issues page. Mention Nonogram Daily and include your device and iOS version, the steps to reproduce the issue, and what happened. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Privacy

Your puzzle progress and statistics are stored on your device. The app does not use ads or analytics. If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple handles your player identity and any leaderboard submissions under its own privacy terms. Read the Nonogram Daily Privacy Policy.
