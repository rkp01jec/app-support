Paw Sort Puzzle · Color sort game

Support

Drag cats and dogs into matching-color baskets. Sort every pet to clear the level.

How to play

Tap a basket to pick up the matching pets on top, then tap another basket to drop them in. Green baskets can take the selected pets; red means the move is not allowed. A basket holds 4 pets — match the color or use an empty basket. Tap the selected basket to cancel. Fewer moves earns more stars, and a new Daily Puzzle arrives every day.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move pets?

Tap a basket to pick up the matching pets at its top, then tap the basket you want to move them to.

What do the green and red baskets mean?

Green baskets can accept the selected pets. A red outline means the move is not allowed — either a different color is on top, or the basket is already full.

How do I undo a move?

Tap the undo arrow at the top of the game screen to take a move back. Restart starts the level over.

What is the Daily Puzzle?

A new puzzle arrives every day. Solve it to keep your streak alive.

How are my progress and stars saved?

Your stars, highest level, and daily streak are saved locally on your device. If you use Game Center, leaderboard scores are submitted to Apple.

How can I report a problem?

Contact support through the repository issues page. Mention Paw Sort Puzzle and include your device and iOS version, the steps to reproduce the issue, and what happened. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Privacy

The game stores level progress, stars, and your daily streak on your device. It does not use ads or analytics. If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple handles your player identity and any leaderboard submissions under its own privacy terms. Read the Paw Sort Puzzle Privacy Policy.
