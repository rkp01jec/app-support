Focus Pet Timer

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This policy describes information handled by Focus Pet Timer, a focus timer and virtual pet app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores focus session dates and durations, selected pet, and pet progress locally using Apple's standard app storage. The developer does not receive this locally stored information. Device backups may include app data according to your device settings.

Apple Game Center

If you use Game Center, the app uses Apple's GameKit to authenticate and submit total focus minutes and best streak scores to the app's leaderboards. Game Center is optional. Apple processes account and leaderboard information under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising, and other data

The app does not include advertising or analytics services and does not operate its own account or data-collection service. It does not request access to contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

Data removal

Removing the app or its local data may remove locally stored session history and pet progress. Device backups are managed through your Apple device settings.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. For privacy or support questions, contact us through the support repository and mention Focus Pet Timer. Please do not include sensitive information.
