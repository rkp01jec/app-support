Set aside time to focus, build a streak, and help your companion pet grow.

Getting started

Choose a preset or tap Custom to set a duration, then tap Start Focus. Pause or resume a session as needed. End early to record completed whole minutes. Visit Pets to choose an unlocked companion; each completed session helps that pet grow.

Frequently asked questions

Which timer durations are available?

Choose 15, 25, 45, or 60 minutes, or choose a custom duration from 5 to 120 minutes in five-minute increments.

What happens if I end a session early?

The app records the whole minutes completed if you focused for at least one minute. Ending early does not record a partial minute.

How do pets unlock and grow?

Pets unlock as your total focus minutes increase. Each pet grows from egg to baby after one session and becomes an adult after three sessions with that pet.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Game Center is optional and is only used for the app's focus-minute and streak leaderboards.

How can I report a problem?

Contact support through the repository issues page. Mention Focus Pet Timer and include your device, iOS version, steps to reproduce, and what happened. Do not include sensitive information.

Privacy

Session history and pet progress are stored locally on your device. Optional Game Center scores are handled by Apple. The app does not use advertising or analytics. Read the Focus Pet Timer Privacy Policy.
