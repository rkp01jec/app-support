Traffic Unjam · Parking puzzle

Support

Slide cars along their lanes, clear a path, and drive the red car through the exit.

How to play

Drag a car along its lane: horizontal cars move left or right, and vertical cars move up or down. Cars cannot pass through each other. Move the red car to the opening on the right to finish the level.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I move a car through another car?

Cars are restricted to their lane and cannot overlap or pass through another car. Move the blocking cars along their lanes to create room.

How do I finish a level?

Clear the lane in front of the red car, then slide it through the exit at the right edge of the board.

How are levels and scores saved?

Your level progress and score are saved locally on your device. If you use Game Center, leaderboard scores are submitted to Apple.

How can I report a problem?

Contact support through the repository issues page. Mention Traffic Unjam and include your device and iOS version, the steps to reproduce the issue, and what happened. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Privacy

The game stores level progress and score on your device. It does not use ads or analytics. If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple handles your player identity and any leaderboard submissions under its own privacy terms. Read the Traffic Unjam Privacy Policy.
