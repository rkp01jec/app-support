Traffic Unjam

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy describes the information handled by Traffic Unjam, a parking-escape puzzle game.

Information stored on your device

Traffic Unjam stores your level progress and score locally on your device using Apple’s standard app storage. The developer does not receive this locally stored game data. Removing the app or its data may remove this information.

Apple Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, the app uses Apple GameKit to authenticate and submit scores and level progress to the game’s leaderboards. Apple processes Game Center account and leaderboard information under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. Game Center is optional.

Analytics, advertising, and other data

Traffic Unjam does not include advertising or analytics services and does not operate its own account or data-collection service. The app does not request access to contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

Children’s privacy

Traffic Unjam does not knowingly collect personal information. Any information associated with an optional Game Center account is handled by Apple under its policies and account settings.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app’s data practices change. For privacy or support questions, contact us through the support repository and mention Traffic Unjam. Please do not include sensitive information.
